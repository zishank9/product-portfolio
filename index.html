A little about me

I bring business and technical teams onto the same page.

My professional journey has taken me through technical support, business analysis and product delivery in enterprise hospitality software. Each stage has helped me understand a different part of the product lifecycle: the customer problem, the business requirement and the work needed to deliver a practical solution.

I enjoy working through complex requirements, asking questions when something is unclear, documenting expected behaviour and helping different teams stay aligned. My work has included data migration, identity management, cloud monitoring, AWS migration and third-party integrations.

I work closely with developers, QA, DevOps, clients and other stakeholders. I can engage with technical details, investigate issues using logs and testing tools, and help clarify problems without confusing delivery coordination with software development ownership.

My approach is practical: understand the problem, make the requirement clear, coordinate the right people, validate the result and support the team through delivery.

01 · Understand the problem

Clarify stakeholder needs, existing workflows, constraints and the intended business outcome.

02 · Make the work actionable

Document requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, dependencies and expected behaviour.

03 · Coordinate delivery

Keep engineering, QA, operations and stakeholders aligned on progress, issues and release readiness.

04 · Validate and improve

Support testing, investigate issues, clarify gaps and help teams prepare for go-live and ongoing operations.
