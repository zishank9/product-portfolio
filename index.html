About Me

My background spans Business Analysis and Product Delivery in enterprise hospitality software. I work at the intersection of business requirements and technical implementation, helping teams understand what needs to be delivered, why it matters and how to validate the result.

My experience includes enterprise data migration, identity integrations, cloud monitoring, AWS environment migration, third-party integrations, testing coordination and go-live support. I collaborate with clients, developers, QA and DevOps teams to clarify requirements, investigate issues and move work towards delivery.

I hold a Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) certification and bring a practical, delivery-focused approach to working with technical and business teams.
