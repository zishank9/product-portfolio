Case Study 05 · Hospitality Integrations

Delivering Hospitality IoT and Third-Party Integrations

How I translated client requirements into integration user stories, coordinated developers and external technology teams, prepared a test environment, and supported integration testing and client delivery.

Role

Product Delivery Manager

Project focus

Hospitality IoT and third-party integrations

Systems involved

PBX, voicemail, Wi-Fi, and external platforms

Delivery scope

Requirements, testing, coordination, and go-live

Project Overview

Hospitality properties often rely on multiple systems working together to support their day-to-day operations. These may include property management software, telephony, voicemail, Wi-Fi services, and other third-party platforms.

My work involved helping deliver integrations between the hospitality platform and external systems. This required understanding client expectations, documenting requirements, coordinating implementation, and validating that integrations behaved as expected before delivery.

The work spanned several integration types and external providers, each with its own requirements and coordination needs.

The Challenge

Third-party integration delivery required business requirements, product behaviour, and external system expectations to be aligned. Issues could arise from unclear requirements, differences between systems, configuration dependencies, or unexpected behaviour during testing.

  • Understand the client's intended workflow and translate it into clear product requirements.
  • Coordinate development work with internal engineers and external technology providers.
  • Establish a suitable test environment for validating integration behaviour.
  • Investigate integration issues and coordinate resolution before delivery.
  • Support go-live and follow-up troubleshooting when required.

My Role and Contributions

1. Requirements gathering and user stories

  • Worked with clients to understand integration requirements and expected business behaviour.
  • Translated requirements into user stories and actionable tasks for the development team.
  • Clarified requirements and answered questions that arose during implementation.
  • Helped align client expectations with the intended behaviour of the integrated systems.

2. Internal and third-party coordination

  • Coordinated with developers and third-party teams to progress integration work.
  • Followed up on dependencies, technical questions, and issues affecting delivery.
  • Helped maintain alignment between the business requirement and the technical implementation.

3. Test-environment setup and validation

  • Set up the IoT test environment required to validate integrations.
  • Tested integration behaviour before delivery to the client.
  • Investigated unexpected results and coordinated with the relevant technical teams to resolve issues.
  • Supported follow-up testing where changes were required.

4. Delivery and post-go-live support

  • Coordinated integration delivery to the client.
  • Supported go-live activities and helped address integration issues when they arose.
  • Worked with developers and external teams during post-delivery troubleshooting.

Integration Areas

My experience covered multiple types of hospitality technology integrations, including:

  • PBX and telephony: Integrations involving hotel phone systems.
  • Voicemail: Integrations involving voicemail functionality.
  • Wi-Fi: Connectivity-related third-party integrations.
  • External hospitality platforms: Integrations involving providers such as Canary, Virdee, Toast, and other third-party systems.

These examples describe the integration areas and providers I worked with; they do not imply that every provider used the same technical implementation or integration workflow.

My Delivery Approach

  1. Understand the workflow: Gather client requirements and identify the expected behaviour of the integration.
  2. Document requirements: Convert the requirements into user stories and clear implementation expectations.
  3. Coordinate implementation: Work with developers and third-party teams to clarify dependencies and progress delivery.
  4. Prepare and test: Set up the test environment, validate behaviour, and investigate discrepancies.
  5. Support delivery: Coordinate client delivery and assist with go-live or post-delivery troubleshooting.

Contribution and Delivery Impact

My contribution helped connect business expectations with technical delivery across multiple integration workstreams.

  • Translated client requirements into structured user stories for implementation.
  • Coordinated internal developers and third-party teams throughout integration delivery.
  • Established a test environment and performed integration validation before delivery.
  • Supported investigation and resolution of integration issues.
  • Helped coordinate client delivery, go-live, and post-delivery troubleshooting.

No numerical performance improvements are claimed because verified measurements are not available for publication.

Key Learnings

  • Integration projects require a shared understanding of expected behaviour across clients, internal developers, and external providers.
  • Clear user stories reduce ambiguity during implementation and testing.
  • A dedicated test environment helps identify integration issues before client delivery.
  • Successful delivery includes coordination and troubleshooting after implementation, not just completing development tasks.
